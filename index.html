SARVO

A membership and community platform for cab and auto drivers.

SARVO is run by a registered partnership firm based in Bengaluru. Drivers join through the SARVO Android app, verify their identity, and take a membership that gives them access to SARVO driver tools and community.

How it works

1. Create your account in the app

Download the SARVO app and sign up with your email or mobile number. We send a one-time code to verify it is you. Login happens inside the SARVO app — we never ask for your code anywhere else.

2. Take a membership

Memberships are purchased at pay.sarvoapp.in by UPI or card. Prices are shown with GST before you pay.

3. Use your membership

Open the app and your membership shows as active, with the date it runs until. Everything your membership unlocks appears there.

Security

SARVO will never ask you for your one-time code by phone, WhatsApp or SMS. Anyone who does is not from SARVO. Enter your code only in the SARVO app.
